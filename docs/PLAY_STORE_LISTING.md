## Full Description
```text
Explore infinite mathematical beauty in real time with Fractal Viewer — a fast, free, and open-source fractal explorer rebuilt from the ground up with GPU shader acceleration and Material You (Material 3 Expressive).

HYBRID GPU + CPU DEEP ZOOM ENGINE
• Real-time GLSL GPU shader (60–120+ FPS) for fluid panning and pinching without lag or excessive battery heat.
• Automatic Dekker-Knuth double-float (df64) GPU emulation seamlessly extends smooth zooming up to ~10^12x magnification.
• Multi-core CPU float64 fallback automatically takes over for ultra-deep exploration up to 10^15x magnification, or switch backends manually at any time (Auto / GPU / CPU).
• Choose your preferred balance of speed and sharpness with 4 quality presets: Performance (0.5x), Balanced (0.75x), Native (1x), and Ultra (2x2 SSAA supersampling), plus automatic or manual iteration limits.

4 CLASSIC FRACTALS & INTERACTIVE PARAMETERS
• Mandelbrot Set (with fast cardioid & period-2 bulb optimization)
• Julia Sets (tweak Re(c) and Im(c) live with interactive sliders)
• Burning Ship Fractal
• Tricorn (Mandelbar)

7 SMOOTH PALETTES & MATERIAL YOU
• System Dynamic palette that adapts directly to your device's Material You wallpaper colors.
• Curated continuous cyclic palettes: Inferno, Viridis, Ocean, Neon, Monochrome, and Twilight.
• Smooth continuous coloring (no harsh contour bands) plus a live Color Phase Shift slider.

BOOKMARKS, TELEMETRY & 4K PNG EXPORT
• Jump right into 9 famous mathematical landmarks (Seahorse Valley, Elephant Valley, Mini-Brot Island, Triple Spiral, Julia Dendrite, and more) or save your own custom bookmarks.
• Precision focal-point gestures keep the spot under your fingers or cursor locked as you zoom, with full keyboard & mouse support on tablets, foldables, and Chromebooks.
• Export and share crisp high-resolution PNG snapshots in Screen (HD), 2K (2560px), or 4K (3840px).
• Optional live telemetry HUD with FPS, frame time, engine mode, and one-tap coordinate copying.

100% FREE & OPEN SOURCE
• Zero ads, zero tracking, works completely offline, and available in English and Ukrainian.
• Source code, releases, and issue tracker are open source on GitHub: https://github.com/Puzzaks/fractals
```
